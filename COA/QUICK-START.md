## 💡 Pro Tips for Maximum Efficiency

### 1. The Pomodoro Technique (25 min focus + 5 min break)
- Study 25 minutes with ZERO distractions
- Break 5 minutes (walk, water, stretch)
- After 4 Pomodoros, take 15-30 min break

### 2. The Feynman Technique (Teach to Learn)
- After each section, explain to imaginary student
- If you can't explain simply, re-read that part
- Record voice explanations (play back later)

### 3. The Interleaving Method (Mix Topics)
- Don't study Module 04 for 30 hours straight
- Mix: 2 hours Module 04, 1 hour Module 07, 1 hour PYQs
- Prevents boredom, enhances retention

### 4. The Testing Effect (Test > Re-reading)
- Solve problems FIRST, then read theory
- Close book, recall from memory
- Test yourself daily on previous topics

### 5. The Spacing Effect (Distributed Practice)
- Review Module 04 on Day 1, 3, 7, 14, 28
- Use exponential spacing (1, 2, 4, 8, 16 days)
- Anki app automates this (optional)

## 🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake 1: Reading Without Deriving
**Wrong:** "I understand the formula, moving on."
**Right:** Close book, derive formula from scratch on paper.

### ❌ Mistake 2: Solving Problems Once
**Wrong:** Solve problem, check answer, next problem.
**Right:** Solve 3 times (day 1, day 3, day 7) until automatic.

### ❌ Mistake 3: Skipping "Easy" Topics
**Wrong:** "I know number systems, skipping Module 01."
**Right:** GATE asks trick questions on basics. Review at least once.

### ❌ Mistake 4: No Error Log
**Wrong:** Make mistake, understand, move on, repeat same mistake later.
**Right:** Maintain error log, review weekly, encode anti-pattern.

### ❌ Mistake 5: Passive Reading
**Wrong:** Read module like a novel, highlight important parts.
**Right:** Active reading - pause, derive, question, test yourself.

### ❌ Mistake 6: Neglecting Mental Models
**Wrong:** Memorize formulas without understanding.
**Right:** Build mental sliders, use mnemonics, visualize in 3D.

### ❌ Mistake 7: Ignoring PYQs
**Wrong:** Focus only on theory and standard problems.
**Right:** PYQs reveal exam patterns. Solve 200+ before GATE.

## 📞 When You Get Stuck

### Doubt Resolution Protocol:
1. **Re-read** that section (80% of doubts resolve)
2. **Derive** from first principles on paper
3. **Check cross-topic bridges** (might be explained in another module)
4. **Search GATE Overflow** (likely someone asked same question)
5. **Ask in forums** (Overflow, Reddit r/GATE, Telegram groups)
6. **Move on temporarily** (come back after 2 days - fresh perspective)

### Never Spend > 30 Minutes on Single Doubt
- Mark it, move forward
- Return when mind is fresh
- Sometimes concepts "click" later
