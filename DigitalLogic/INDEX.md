## 📖 Complete Module Curriculum (9 Modules)

### **Phase 1: Foundation (Modules 01-03) - 12 hours**

#### Module 01: Number Systems & Codes
- Binary, Octal, Hexadecimal, BCD, Gray code
- Number system conversions
- Signed representations (1's, 2's complement)
- Floating-point numbers
- **Time**: 3 hours | **Lines**: 800

#### Module 02: Boolean Algebra
- Logic gates (AND, OR, NOT, XOR, NAND, NOR, XNOR)
- Boolean theorems and laws
- De Morgan's theorem
- Algebraic simplification
- **Time**: 4 hours | **Lines**: 778

#### Module 03: Minimization Techniques
- Karnaugh Maps (2, 3, 4, 5 variable)
- Don't care conditions
- Quine-McCluskey method
- Prime implicants
- **Time**: 5 hours | **Lines**: 942

---

### **Phase 2: Core Implementation (Modules 04-05) - 18 hours**

#### ⭐ Module 04: Combinational Circuits (NEW)
- **Multiplexers (MUX)**: 2:1 to 16:1, tree implementations
- **Demultiplexers (DEMUX)**: Data routing, memory addressing
- **Encoders & Decoders**: Priority, cascading, gate counts
- **Adders & Subtractors**: Half/Full adder, ripple carry, CLA
- **Comparators**: Equality, magnitude, cascading
- **Parity Circuits**: Error detection, Hamming codes
- **Code Converters**: Gray-Binary, BCD conversions
- **Quick Formulas & Tricks**
- **Previous Year Patterns**
- **Time**: 8 hours | **Lines**: 1,017

#### ⭐ Module 05: Sequential Circuits (NEW)
- **Latches**: SR (NOR/NAND), D latch, metastability
- **Flip-Flops**: D, JK, T, SR flip-flops
- **Master-Slave Architecture**: Synchronous design
- **Setup/Hold/Propagation Times**: Timing constraints
- **Flip-Flop Conversions**: Matrix with gate counts
- **Registers**: Basic, shift (SIPO, SISO, PISO), universal
- **Counters**: Ripple, synchronous, MOD-N, ring, Johnson
- **State Machines**: Mealy vs Moore, FSM synthesis
- **Sequence Detectors**: Design patterns
- **Time**: 10 hours | **Lines**: 968

---

### **Phase 3: Advanced Topics (Modules 06-07) - 8 hours**

#### ⭐ Module 06: Logic Families (NEW)
- **TTL (Transistor-Transistor Logic)**
  - Logic levels, subfamilies (S, LS, ALS)
  - Noise margin, fan-out, propagation delay
  - Power dissipation analysis
  
- **CMOS (Complementary MOS)**
  - Logic levels, subfamilies (HC, HCT, LVC)
  - Low static power, high dynamic power
  - Temperature effects
  
- **ECL (Emitter-Coupled Logic)**
  - Current steering, fastest logic
  - High power consumption, temperature sensitive
  
- **Advanced Parameters**
  - Noise sources & margin calculations
  - Decoupling capacitors
  - Fan-in/fan-out effects
  - Power dissipation breakdown
  
- **Time**: 4 hours | **Lines**: 708

#### ⭐ Module 07: Memory & PLD (NEW)
- **Memory Organization**: 1D, 2D, 3D arrays
- **SRAM**: 6T cells, bistable latches, fastest RAM
- **DRAM**: 1T1C cell, refresh mechanism, most used
- **ROM Types**: Masked, PROM, EPROM, EEPROM
- **Flash Memory**: NOR, NAND, SLC/MLC/TLC/QLC
- **PLA & PAL**: Programmable logic arrays
- **FPGA**: LUT, CLB, programmable interconnect
- **Capacity Calculations**: Address/data lines
- **Time**: 4 hours | **Lines**: 756
